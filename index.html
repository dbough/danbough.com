Coaching

Coaching people through growth and change

I'm a trained Co-Active coach. I help people grow — whatever's going on in their life. Career changes, personal development, feeling stuck, or wanting to do deeper work on themselves.

Who I work with

  • People in a career transition, job loss, or a rough work environment
  • People who pour themselves into caring for others
  • Anyone looking for more meaning, or feeling a little lost
  • People who want deeper work on themselves but aren't sure where to start
  • Anyone facing a health challenge and figuring out what life looks like now

What we dig into

  • Where you're fulfilled — and where you're not
  • Your values, and how they actually show up in your life
  • Living with self-authority and self-acceptance
  • What really lights you up, and how to make more room for it
