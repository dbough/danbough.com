What's included

Practical AI guardrails

Everything you need to establish a clear foundation for responsible AI use, especially if your small business or nonprofit is starting from scratch.

  • Acceptable Use Policy

    Clear, plain-language guidance on how your organization can and can't use AI.

    • Approved and prohibited uses
    • Human review expectations
    • Staff and volunteer responsibilities

    Defines practical boundaries for everyday AI use, including when AI is appropriate, when human review is required, and what staff and volunteers are responsible for when using AI-generated content.

  • AI Tool Approval Process

    A simple, repeatable way to evaluate new AI tools before they're introduced.

    • Privacy and security review
    • Data-use considerations
    • Approval and ownership

    Creates a consistent process for reviewing new tools so decisions aren't made ad hoc. It helps your organization consider risk, data practices, organizational need, ownership, and approval before adoption.

  • Data Handling Guidelines

    Clear guidance for deciding what information is safe to share with AI tools.

    • Sensitive and confidential data
    • Donor, member, customer, and employee information
    • Practical do/don't examples

    Helps staff and volunteers understand what information can safely be entered into AI tools, what should never be shared, and how to handle donor, member, customer, employee, financial, proprietary, and other sensitive information.
